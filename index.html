Why And When To Use Waterproofing Product

New Construction

For new constructions, incorporating waterproofing products from the beginning is essential. This proactive approach prevents water ingress from the outset, safeguarding the building's structure and interior spaces.

KNOW MORE

Repair/Renovation:

Cracks, gaps, and weakened surfaces can compromise the integrity of your home's waterproofing. In such cases, using waterproofing products during repairs or renovations becomes crucial.

How We Work

Six stages. Photographed at every step.

Waterproofing fails when a step is skipped. We run the same sequence on a ₹8,000 bathroom and a 40,000 sq.ft. terrace.

01Diagnosis

Site Inspection & Leak Tracing

Moisture mapping and a physical walk-through to find the actual source- not just the visible damp patch - before we quote anything.

02Prep

Surface Preparation

Loose plaster, old paint and laitance are removed by grinding or chipping so the new system bonds to sound substrate.

03Repair

Crack & Joint Treatment

Cracks are routed and filled, expansion joints resealed, and drain outlets rebuilt before any membrane goes down.

04Apply

Waterproofing System Application

Primer, reinforcing fabric where needed, and the specified number of coats- applied to manufacturer DFT, not eyeballed.

05Verify

Curing & Ponding Test

A minimum 48–72 hour water ponding test on horizontal surfaces before we call a job complete.

06Close-out

Warranty & Handover

A written warranty, the site photo log, and material batch numbers handed over with the final invoice.
